# Who Is Lily?
Maybe what they were really interested in was not simply who Lily is, but how closely Lily and I are connected.

So, for one week, from Monday to Sunday, I tracked our daily activities across several things: sleeping hours, water consumed, drama-watching hours, shopping items, screen time, book reading, and words spoken.

I collected the numbers, put them into a dataset, and calculated the correlation between Lily’s data and mine across each activity.

Instead of answering with a sentence, I answered with seven days of data.
